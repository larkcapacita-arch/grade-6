What your score means

Score guide for grades 6–10

Crushing it
80–100
Your brain skills are strong! You handle tasks, stay focused, and manage your time well. Keep using what works and share your tricks with friends.
On your way
60–79
You've got a solid foundation but some things get tricky, especially when school gets busy or stressful. Learning a few strategies can help a lot.
Needs a boost
40–59
Several areas are making school harder than it needs to be. It's worth talking to a teacher, counselor, or parent — they have tools that can genuinely help.
Getting tough
20–39
These challenges are significantly affecting your school life. Please reach out to a counselor or trusted adult — you don't have to deal with this alone.
Need support now
0–19
You're having a really hard time, and that matters. Please talk to a counselor, parent, or teacher right away. There's a lot they can do to help you.

Here's what we suggest for you

Heads up: This check-in is just a starting point — it's not a diagnosis or a final grade. Lots of students in grades 6–10 struggle with these skills, especially when school gets harder. If your score is low, it might be linked to ADHD, anxiety, or just not having learned these skills yet — all of which can be helped. Use this to start a conversation with someone you trust.
